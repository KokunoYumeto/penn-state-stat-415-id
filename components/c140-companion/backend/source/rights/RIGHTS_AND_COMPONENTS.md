# Rights and component register

## Penn State prose and instructional graphics

The frozen landing page identifies the notes as designed and developed by Penn
State's Department of Statistics and offered as open educational resources
under CC BY-NC 4.0. The landing page and Lesson 00 footer both state that,
except where otherwise noted, content on the site is licensed under CC BY-NC
4.0. Neither frozen page contains an individual-author field, page-specific
exception, third-party credit, courtesy/source note, or separate asset licence.

This edition therefore attributes Penn State's Department of Statistics,
links the official course and CC BY-NC 4.0, identifies the Indonesian
translation/reconstruction and changes, remains noncommercial, and does not
imply endorsement. The 13 landing-card course graphics are direct
instructional content with no visible exception or embedded rights statement;
they are frozen under the page-level notice. Their exact identities are in
`authority/FIRST_UNIT_ASSET_MANIFEST.csv`.

## Excluded or separately treated shared chrome

The following directly referenced shared assets are not redistributed in the
reader:

- `PSU_Ecosshield.png` and `assets/psulion.png`: Penn State marks. The linked
  University copyright page directs non-University users of Penn State logos
  to University Licensing; the reader uses plain-text attribution instead.
- `fourfifteen_banner.png`: an identifiable-person course banner with no
  visible model-release/publicity-rights evidence; the reader uses its own
  neutral title surface.
- `assets/by-nc.svg`: the reader uses a text licence link rather than copying
  the badge.
- upstream Quarto/Bootstrap/search/cookie/analytics libraries: unnecessary for
  the normalized offline reader and excluded. No analytics or cookie code is
  carried into the edition.

## Other collection components

The exact Random completeness donor is now present at
`components/random-completeness/`. It preserves the official landing-page CC
BY 2.0 statement and the conflicting Credits-page CC BY 1.0 link, attribution
to Kyle Siegrist and *Random*, modification and non-endorsement notices, and a
separate Apache-2.0 MathJax licence. It is not relabelled as Penn State
content. The original rigor/simulation/regression/mastery companion is CC
BY-SA 4.0 and is never presented as Penn State prose. The C140 collection
preserves these component identities; it has no single uniform licence.

## Capstone datasets

CP01 uses I-Cheng Yeh's *Concrete Compressive Strength*, UCI Machine Learning
Repository, DOI `10.24432/C5PK67`, under CC BY 4.0. The retained attribution is
“Yeh, I. (1998). Concrete Compressive Strength [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning
Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5PK67.” Raw bytes, archive members, and
faithful data derivatives retain CC BY 4.0 and its attribution obligations;
they are not relicensed as companion CC BY-SA. The frozen rights and byte
evidence is `components/c140-companion/data/capstones/CP01/DATASET_PROVENANCE.json`.

transmitters on survival, nesting propensity, and nest success of greater
sage-grouse*, DOI `10.5061/dryad.573n5tbf3`, version 3 / version ID `268230`,
under CC0-1.0. Raw and faithful derivative data remain CC0 and are not
relicensed as companion CC BY-SA. The frozen version, rights, and byte evidence
is in `components/c140-companion/data/capstones/CP02/DATASET_PROVENANCE.json`
and `components/c140-companion/data/capstones/CP02/RIGHTS_EVIDENCE.md`.
