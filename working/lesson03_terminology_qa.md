# Lesson 03 terminology QA — Indonesian mathematical statistics

Checked: 2026-08-25

## Evidence and continuity

This decision reuses the exact Indonesian mathematical-statistics evidence
already frozen for the component, including the representative Indonesian
university/journal sources recorded in the Lesson 01 and Lesson 02 terminology
notes. No new unresolved field term required a broader search. The controlling
distinctions remain `penduga` versus `nilai dugaan`, `nilai harapan` versus
`rataan`, and `varians` rather than alternating with `ragam` inside one reader.

## Decisions

- `sufficiency` is **kecukupan**; a `sufficient statistic` is **statistik
  cukup**, and “sufficient for theta” is **cukup bagi theta**.
- `Factorization Theorem` is **Teorema Faktorisasi**. A joint density/mass is
  **fungsi kepadatan/massa peluang bersama**.
- `one-to-one function` is **fungsi satu-satu**. `Bijektif` is avoided because
  the source asserts injectivity, not surjectivity onto an independently fixed
  codomain.
- `conditional distribution` is **distribusi bersyarat**; when the conditioning
  object must be explicit, use **distribusi bersyarat dengan syarat Y**.
- `exponential form`, `Exponential Criterion`, and `exponential family` are
  respectively **bentuk eksponensial**, **Kriteria Eksponensial**, and
  **keluarga eksponensial**. These must remain distinct from the exponential
  distribution.
- A pair of `jointly sufficient statistics` is rendered in prose as **pasangan
  statistik yang secara bersama-sama cukup**, avoiding an ambiguous compound.
- `method of moments estimator` is **penduga metode momen**; `theoretical
  moment`, `sample moment`, and `population moment` are **momen teoretis**,
  **momen sampel**, and **momen populasi**.
- `moment about the origin` is **momen terhadap titik asal**. `Moment about the
  mean` is **momen terhadap rataan**, with **momen pusat** introduced as its
  concise standard alias.
- `empirical distribution`, `gamma function`, and `likelihood function` are
  **distribusi empiris**, **fungsi gamma**, and **fungsi kemungkinan**.
