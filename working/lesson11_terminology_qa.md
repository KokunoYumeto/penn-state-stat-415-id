# Lesson 11 terminology QA

The cumulative id-ID glossary now extends through `O006-TERM-0168`. Lesson 11
uses the established distinctions `peluang`, `fungsi kemungkinan`, `penduga`,
`nilai dugaan`, and `selang kredibel`, and adds the Bayesian terms recorded in
rows 0151–0168.

Controlling choices include `pendekatan frekuentis`, `peluang subjektif`,
`distribusi prior`, `distribusi posterior`, `fungsi kepadatan posterior`,
`penduga Bayes`, `fungsi kerugian`, `kerugian galat kuadrat`, `kerugian galat
absolut`, `rataan posterior`, `median posterior`, and `selang kredibel berekor
sama`. Discrete laws use `fungsi massa peluang`; continuous laws use `fungsi
kepadatan`. The source's English word *likelihood* remains `fungsi kemungkinan`
and is never used as a synonym for an event probability.

The previous Indonesian-field terminology audit remains controlling. No new
external terminology source was needed for this bounded lesson.
